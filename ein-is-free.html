Pre-flight

The EIN is free. The mill is the product.

If you formed a U.S. LLC this month, you do not need to pay anyone for an Employer Identification Number. The IRS still issues them at no charge on IRS.gov.

A page that charges $49, $79, or $199 to “file your EIN today” is not faster than the IRS in any way that matters. It is a form-filler standing between you and a free government site.

What an EIN is for

The EIN is the federal identifier for the company. Banks, vendors, and payroll use it. It is not a credit line. It is not a D-U-N-S Number. It does not report to Equifax on its own. It is a number on a notice, in the exact legal name of the LLC.

How to file it without buying a mill

  1. Open the official IRS EIN application. Not a search ad. Not a “fast EIN” domain.
  2. Use the legal name exactly as it appears on the state formation document. Do not retype from memory. Do not expand “and” into “&” unless the state filing already did.
  3. The IRS requires a responsible party. That is a real principal of the company. Ignition does not collect Social Security numbers and you should not paste one into a chat box on a third-party site.
  4. Save the issuance notice as a PDF next to the articles of organization. You will need both at the bank.

What actually delays a new LLC

Not the EIN. These do:

  • The name on the EIN does not match the name on the state filing.
  • The address on the bank account does not match the address on the first vendor application.
  • Someone sold you a “faster DUNS” product and you now have two versions of the company in circulation.
  • You asked a personal banker for a business loan on the same day you opened checking, and you left with a consumer product in a principal’s name.
