Identity

Request a D-U-N-S Number without buying a reseller.

Dun & Bradstreet files companies under a D-U-N-S Number. A new U.S. LLC can request one at no charge on dnb.com.

Issuance is often measured in weeks, not hours. That wait is real. It is also not a reason to pay a reseller for a “same-week DUNS.”

What the number is

It is an identifier in D&B’s commercial file. Some vendors look at that file when they decide whether to invoice a new company. Some do not. Having a DUNS request on file is useful. Having a Paydex score in week one is not a promise anyone honest will make.

The free path

  1. Finish the boring identity work first: exact legal name, one address you can receive a package at, one business phone, EIN in that same name.
  2. Request the number on dnb.com. Use the same name, address, and phone you already locked.
  3. Save the confirmation. Do not buy an add-on during the request unless you independently want that product for a reason that is not “a YouTube video said so.”
  4. Wait. Follow up only through D&B’s own channels.

Why resellers make the file worse

A faster-DUNS shop is a second pair of hands typing your company into a bureau. If they format the name differently, use a mailbox you do not use elsewhere, or attach a phone that is not the business phone, you have not accelerated the file. You have forked it.

Ignition’s Sprint Pass guarantee is void if you buy a paid DUNS product. That is not a moral lecture. Forked identity is how on-time applications still come back as “we cannot find this company.”

Where this sits on the clock

On the Sprint Pass, the free DUNS request is due by day 4 after the business checking account opens. The guarantee is about written vendor net-30 terms in the LLC name, not about the calendar date D&B publishes a score.
