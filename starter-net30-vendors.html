Trade credit

What new LLCs actually get from starter net-30 vendors.

A brand-new company does not get a bank loan because articles of organization exist. What it can sometimes get, if the identity is clean, is narrower and more useful: a supplier that invoices the LLC and gives the company time to pay.

That is trade credit. It is not a FICO score. It is not a Paydex date. It is a vendor saying the company may pay on terms.

What “net-30” means here

Net-30 means the invoice is due in 30 days. If the vendor writes those terms in the LLC’s legal name, the company has an open trade line with that vendor. Whether and when a bureau posts that line is a separate, slower event. Ignition’s guarantee, when you buy the Sprint Pass, is about the vendor’s terms — not about a score appearing on a report.

What to expect from common starter vendors

These names circulate because they still sell goods a new company can actually use, and because they have, at times, granted invoice terms to young entities. None of them have pre-approved your LLC. Policies change. Read the vendor’s own application.

  • Uline. Often the most reachable packing-and-shipping house. A first order may be prepaid. Terms, if they come, sometimes arrive after the company has paid one invoice like an adult. An account login is not net-30.
  • Quill. Office supply. Net-30 is commonly requested at checkout, sometimes with a merchandise minimum. Reporting to a commercial bureau, when it happens, is often an opt-in and can lag weeks after the invoice. Ask the vendor, not a thread from 2019.
  • Grainger. Industrial and MRO, not a “starter tradeline factory.” Some files need an ordinary customer account before anyone will talk about invoice terms. A two-week-old LLC with no purchases can get “not yet.” “Not yet” is not an open line.

Why clean files get terms and messy files get a card form

  • The legal name matches the state, the EIN, the bank, and the application. Not almost. Exactly.
  • The address can receive a package.
  • The phone is the same phone everywhere.
  • The first order is paid from the LLC checking account, not a personal card that makes the vendor treat you as a consumer.

Most “we don’t approve new companies” letters are NAP letters in nicer language.

What this page will not give you

It will not give you paste-ready applications, suggested cart contents, or a script for the credit department. Those packets are the Sprint Pass, because they have to be right on a specific Tuesday.

It will also not tell you that any vendor has guaranteed your LLC. Ignition’s own guarantee is a refund on the Sprint Pass if you run the dated checklist on time and still have no written vendor net-30 terms by day 30. That is a refund. It is not Uline’s letterhead.
