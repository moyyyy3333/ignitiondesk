Scope

What Ignition is, precisely

Stated plainly, because this category is full of businesses that blur it.

Ignition is document-preparation and scheduling software for newly formed U.S. business entities. It generates business paperwork — operating agreement drafts, vendor trade-account applications, a standardized business name and address block — and gives you a dated checklist you complete yourself.

Ignition is not credit repair, credit monitoring, or credit counseling. We do not dispute, correct, or attempt to alter anything on any credit report. We do not monitor credit files. We are not a lender and do not broker or arrange financing. We never request your Social Security number and never touch your personal credit — our customer is the business entity, not the consumer.
